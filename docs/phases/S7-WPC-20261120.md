## Delivered behaviour
- **Risk matrix decision:** there is no built-in matrix. A tenant `RiskMethod` holds the likelihood codes, severity codes and every L×S cell exactly once (`method_matrix_incomplete` otherwise). An author creates it and a different person with `hse.risk_method.publish` publishes it. The DB rejects `PublishedBy = ActorId`. Ratings are lookups only: `METHOD_UNCONFIGURED` with no published method, `NOT_ASSESSED` when a code is missing. Nothing is seeded, so every rating stays unconfigured until a person publishes a method.
- **DEV-105 assessment** (`/api/v1/hse/risk-assessments`, vessel scoped):
  - Content covers hazards, consequences, initial and residual likelihood/severity with rationale, controls (kind, owner, required), barrier references, stop-work (UNKNOWN until declared, never a silent "no") and participants.
  - Pinned links to TugJob, WorkOrder, Defect or OperationalEvent (downtime) record the exact revision visible to the actor; a later change shows as CURRENT, STALE or UNAVAILABLE.
  - A template can be applied as a copy, with no rating, owner, participant or approval carried over.
  - Lifecycle: DRAFT/RETURNED → submit (complete fields required, 422 blocker codes otherwise) → IN_REVIEW → APPROVE/RETURN as a human decision record with a reason. The approver may not be an author of the cycle or its submitter.
  - Approved revisions are immutable. Amend opens a new DRAFT revision that must be reviewed again.
  - Acknowledgements are per person, ACK or DISPUTE, on the approved revision only, and only by a participant. A dispute is never closed by someone else.
- **DEV-106 template library** (`/api/v1/hse/risk-templates`, tenant scope):
  - Fields: work type, vessel/tug scope, revision, effective-from, hazards, default controls, required barriers, approval owner, change reason, source refs.
  - Lifecycle: DRAFT → IN_REVIEW → PUBLISH or RETURN as an HSEQ human decision by a non-author. Publishing supersedes the previously effective revision, which is retained. A future-dated revision never supersedes the current one early (CURRENT/SCHEDULED is derived).
  - Revise creates a new draft revision.
  - Crew suggestions are counted (OPEN + ACCEPTED). Accepting a suggestion never edits or publishes the template.
  - `GET /risk-templates/applicable` lists current published templates that apply to the vessel kind, marked `TEMPLATE_COPY_REQUIRES_REVIEW`.
- **Platform:**
  - 15 commands, each with S1 CriticalWrite scope (+15), `NATIVE-COMMAND-1` profiles and receipt idempotency (`RiskCommandReceipts`, 409 `idempotency_key_reused`). xmin/hash concurrency, SERIALIZABLE transactions classified through `PostgresFailure`, CSRF, strict JSON.
  - Closed `AuditMetadata` projections (codes and revisions only; reason, hazard and suggestion text is withheld). SecurityEventCodes are registered and the client audit vocabulary is updated.
  - 10 new `hse.*` permissions.
  - Server menu destinations `risk-assessment` and `risk-template-library`, plus lens and search entries.
  - OpenAPI and `api.d.ts` regenerated.
  - Migration `20261120030000_S7WpcRiskAssessment`: 10 tables in schema `hse`, CHECK constraints for the human gates.
  - Optional idempotent `RiskFixtures`: FICTIONAL DRAFTs only. No method, publication, decision, acknowledgement or receipt is seeded, and CHECK constraints forbid synthetic approved or published rows.
  - UI: `RiskAssessmentWorkspace` and `RiskTemplateLibraryWorkspace` with loading, empty, error, offline, conflict, stale, incomplete and forbidden states, a same-key retry while the outcome is unknown, and copy in 17 locales.
