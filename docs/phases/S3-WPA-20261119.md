### Expert decision needed (open, not resolved by software)

- **EXPERT-DECISION S3-WPA-LIFECYCLE-AGG-01**: how the lifecycle of a requirement bound by several rules should be aggregated when the rules disagree (for example one IN_FORCE and one DRAFT). 18.01 `Backlog/row:27` names the states but gives no aggregation rule. Until a regulatory or compliance expert decides, the projection publishes the software precedence value together with `MIXED_QUALIFICATION_STATES`. It is not a legal ruling or sign-off, and no consumer may treat the value as authoritative.
- The 8 h envelope TTL and the 100-rule projection limit are software choices that need product and privacy review.

### Residual risk

It is not encrypted. Sign-out is blocked by the existing navigation guard while an UNKNOWN attempt holds `onSaving`. The sign-out purge therefore covers records of views that are not mounted.
