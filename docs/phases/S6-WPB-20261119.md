## OCR approach and provider decision

`CertificateExtraction:Provider` = `ExternalHttp` (default, pre-existing) or `LocalTextLayer` (new).
LocalTextLayer is an in-process, dependency-free reader of the embedded text layer of digital PDFs
(Flate, object streams, ToUnicode CMaps, TJ kerning, cm/Tm transforms) plus deterministic label rules.
No new package, no network, no secret: the policy rejects a local provider that carries an endpoint,
host or secret reference, and requires ModelVersion/PromptVersion to equal the actual engine/rule-set
versions. Basis: docs/dependency-policy.md (no unreviewed dependency) and docs/security-baseline.md
(no unapproved processor, no document content in logs/audit). **Image OCR (PNG/JPEG, scanned PDFs,
Identity-H without ToUnicode, encrypted PDFs) is an explicit UNAVAILABLE capability**, never an empty
success. Neither provider is enabled by default; enabling requires the full privacy/retention/processing
references and per-tenant/actor/vessel scopes already required by policy.

Confidence values are fixed rule scores (0.92 same-line, 0.78 next-line, ≤0.85 normalised date,
0.50 unparsed date, 0.40 ambiguous), **not calibrated probabilities**.
