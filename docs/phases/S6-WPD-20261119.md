## Follow-up: truncation and tank basis (19.11.2026)

- The ledger and timeline keep a bound of the newest 100 heads, but they now detect when older records exist (one extra row is read). In that case the response sets `truncated=true` and adds the `HISTORY_TRUNCATED` qualification. The first in-window record of each fuel type gets continuity `UNKNOWN` with reason `HISTORY_TRUNCATED` and basis `HISTORY_TRUNCATED`. The cut-off boundary is never shown as continuous or as a gap (`ShipOperationsLedger.AssessFuel(records, historyTruncated)`). A complete history keeps `NO_PREVIOUS_RECORD_IN_WINDOW` for the first record.
- Every fuel assessment carries `basis: [FUEL_TYPE_AGGREGATE, TANK_REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE]`, and `TANK_REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE` is in the response qualifications. The UI says that a multi-tank gap is a review question caused by missing tank data, not a data error. It also shows a boundary note on truncated entries. Two keys were added in all 17 languages.
- The client decoder rejects a boundary marked continuous or as a gap, a `HISTORY_TRUNCATED` reason without a truncated response, and a dropped tank basis.
