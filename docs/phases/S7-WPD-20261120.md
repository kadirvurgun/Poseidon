## Delivered behavior

- **Routes** (`DirectoryRead` + `certificate.read`, vessel-scoped, `no-store`). Reads are pre/post-audit fenced: `GET /api/v1/pre-survey-checklists[/capabilities|/port-calls|/{id}|/{id}/history]` and `GET /api/v1/survey-planner?from&to&vesselId` (span ≤ 731 days, ≤ 50 vessels, ≤ 200 rows, truncation disclosed).
- **Writes** are SERIALIZABLE, use body `expectedVersion`+`expectedRevision`, require antiforgery and an actor `Idempotency-Key` receipt, and pass a fresh-scope fence. Each one appends one immutable revision: `POST` create (`certificate.survey.plan`), `PUT /{id}` update, and `POST /{id}/verifications` (Human, `certificate.survey.review`).
- **Persistence:** migration `20261120040000_S7WpdPreSurveyChecklist` adds 3 tables in `assurance` with check constraints, FKs to the exact certificate revision, and DB immutability triggers. The DbContext enforces append-only rules, and AuditMetadata keeps a closed projection (content withheld).
- **Contracts:** 9 endpoint profiles; 3 S1 critical ops (163/154/164). Actions are `PreSurveyRead/PreSurveyCommand/SurveyPlannerRead`, and 5 new result codes were added. The two permissions are in `TrustedPermissions` and the audit capabilities. The client audit vocabulary is updated. OpenAPI was exported (312 paths / 456 schemas) and `api.d.ts` regenerated.
- **Navigation:** the server menu shows `pre-survey-checklist` and `survey-planner` only under `certificate.read`. Lens and search decoders were updated.
- **UI:** `SurveyWorkspace` (planner calendar by month plus an UNKNOWN group; checklist list/create/detail) and `PreSurveyChecklistPanel`. They cover loading, offline, denied, empty, stale, conflict and unknown-outcome states (same-key retry). After a receipt, a fresh GET runs. There is no green or ready tone anywhere. 102 copy keys and 2 nav pairs were added in all 17 locales.
- **Seed:** `PreSurveyChecklistFixtures.AddMissingAsync` is an optional administrative fixture. It creates one FICTIONAL `SyntheticUnverified` checklist per vessel with all items OPEN, and seeds no owner, evidence, plan or verification. Repeats are idempotent.

## Human gates and risks

- The templates are product starters. Approved class/flag checklists need template governance and expert sign-off, which this package does not provide.
- Window dates are the certificate's declared dates, not a source-policy calculation. Because no survey execution or satisfaction record exists, a window that has passed shows as OVERDUE with that qualifier.
- Port-call pins link draft, unverified SOF records. The port identity and the voyage remain unresolved.
- Shared files that need merge attention with WP-A/B/C: `SecurityEventCodes.cs`, `AuditMetadata.cs`, `PoseidonDbContext.cs`, the model snapshot, `FoundationIdentity.cs`, `endpoint-profiles.v1.json`, `s1-current-scope.v1.json` plus `S1ScopeCoverageTests` counts, `EndpointProfileRegistryTests`, the audit vocabulary/capabilities, nav files, OpenAPI/`api.d.ts`, and the 17 locale catalogs.
- `docs/project-status.*` was deliberately left for the bundle integration.
