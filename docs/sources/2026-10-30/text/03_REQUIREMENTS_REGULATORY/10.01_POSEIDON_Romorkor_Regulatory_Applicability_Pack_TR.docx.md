15. Development Acceptance Criteria

## paragraph:289

Tug Job Assignment cannot produce READY when crew/equipment/certificate/PMS critical blocker exists.

## paragraph:290

Shore planner cannot override Master/Tug Master onboard safety gate.

## paragraph:291

Bollard pull value keeps source/provenance/test date; unverified value is not treated as trusted evidence.

## paragraph:292

Emergency release test/defect state is traceable to equipment, job readiness and decision.

## paragraph:293

Harbour/close-quarters and ocean towage profiles can activate different rule packs.

## paragraph:294

Tug-specific records support offline capture where operationally required; material conflicts require human resolution.

## paragraph:295

Every critical readiness state exposes Why → source/control/evidence/blocker/owner/next action.

## paragraph:296

Port management capabilities cannot be reached from Tug Mode even when location/berth is stored as job context.

## paragraph:297

Regulatory rows with unresolved authoritative applicability remain REVIEW_REQUIRED, not silently compliant.

17. Sonuç

## paragraph:322

Römorkör artık POSEIDON içinde bir 'profil' değil, tam operasyon modudur. Regülasyon motoru ortak çekirdeği korurken tug-specific job, readiness, towing equipment, crew competence ve towage execution rule pack'lerini bağlama göre aktive etmelidir. Belirsiz resmi/klas uygulanabilirlik otomatik uyum sonucu üretmemeli; REVIEW_REQUIRED olarak yetkili insan incelemesine gitmelidir.
