## paragraph:294

## paragraph:501

Verified MSMD yoksa römorkör crew readiness full READY olamaz.

## paragraph:502

Expired/unverified required credential NOT_ELIGIBLE veya REVIEW_REQUIRED üretir.

## paragraph:504

Familiarization pending ise newly assigned crew full tug readiness'e katkı vermez.

## paragraph:505

İstanbul/Çanakkale escort context source rule active ise ek crew requirement uygulanır; source version audit edilir.

## paragraph:506

Bollard-pull test/certificate >5-year or unverified ise TR service profile VALID sayılmaz.

## paragraph:507

M79 applicable + emergency release failed ise Tug Equipment Readiness NOT_READY olur.

## paragraph:508

Shore assignment hiçbir durumda Master/Tug Master final onboard safety kararını bypass etmez.

## paragraph:509

Harbour and ocean towage different applicability packs activate without leaking Port Management functionality.
