11. v46.3 System / State Requirements

## paragraph:312

Noon/Deck/Engine logs, inspections and tug execution use the same durable offline command/idempotency/conflict contract.

## paragraph:313

Fuel/BDN and survey evidence is captured as evidence objects; verification is independent from record completion.

## paragraph:314

Port Activity/SOF cannot introduce berth allocation, port traffic or terminal resource entities.

## paragraph:315

Loss-of-Hire is a candidate/commercial-impact attribute; final claim/finance decision remains external/human.

## paragraph:316

Risk template publishing is versioned; published revision is immutable and superseded by new revision.

## paragraph:317

High-Risk Work is a projection, not a duplicate system-of-record.

## paragraph:318

Critical WO postponement and failed survey-prep item can recalculate readiness.
