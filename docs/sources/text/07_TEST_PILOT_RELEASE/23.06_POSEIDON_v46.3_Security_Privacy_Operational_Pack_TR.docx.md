3. Privacy operations

## paragraph:44

Processing inventory: data category, purpose, legal basis/contractual context, owner, recipient/provider, retention.

## paragraph:45

Crew medical data: software only minimum fitness/validity information needed for eligibility unless explicit lawful business need exists.

## paragraph:46

Data-subject/customer requests: identity verify → scope → legal/contract review → search/export/rectify/restrict/delete where applicable → audit.

## paragraph:47

Retention: legal/contract/evidence needs by record type; blanket indefinite retention avoided.

## paragraph:48

Deletion/expiry: evidence under legal hold or audit requirement is not silently purged.

## paragraph:49

International/provider transfer and subprocessor decisions require legal/privacy review.

4. Secure file/evidence operations

## paragraph:51

## paragraph:54

## paragraph:73
