## The six steps
Every page in POSEIDON follows the same working model (13.03 §1): **Today → exception → Why → controlled action → evidence → verification**.
1. **Confirm the context.** Check the company, vessel or tug and your role first.
2. **Handle exceptions on Today.** The Today page lists only the records that need an action or a decision.
3. **Open Why.** When a state is READY, CONDITIONAL, NOT_READY or REVIEW_REQUIRED, open the Why view to see the rule, the source record and any unknown inputs.
4. **Take a controlled action.** Act on the record's own page, within your permissions. Commands are sent once, with a receipt.
5. **Attach evidence.** Where evidence is needed, attach the document, photo, measurement or record.
6. **Wait for verification.** Completing a record and verifying it are different acts. If an authorized verifier is required, the record waits for that person.

## Principles you will meet on every page
- **Least privilege.** You only see and do what your role allows for this company, vessel and record. The onboard safety authority of the Master and Tug Master is always kept.
- **Completed ≠ Verified ≠ Effective.** A finished task is not yet verified; a verified action is not yet proven effective.
- **Evidence is never changed silently.** A new record that replaces an older one is linked as "superseded"; the original stays.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** A missing value is shown as Unknown or Review required. It is never read as "No", "Ready" or "Not applicable".
- **Offline first, no silent merge.** Critical commands wait in a durable queue while offline. A material conflict always needs a person to resolve it.
- **AI only advises.** Detect, explain, recommend; an authorized human decides. AI help is turned off in this release.
