## What to do and whom to tell
This list follows the role-based handbook (13.03 §10).
- **Offline:** continue the critical work and watch the sync state. A material conflict goes to the **Material conflict center**.
- **Access denied:** check your role, vessel and record context. If it is still wrong, contact your **tenant administrator**.
- **Unexpected readiness result:** open the **Why** view. If the rule or the source looks wrong, pass it to the **control owner or subject-matter expert**.
- **A record cannot be verified:** check the evidence and its origin. The **verifier** decides.
- **Application error:** note the correlation ID, the screen, the time and your steps, and send them to **support / engineering**.

## Good habits
- Never work around a refusal by asking a colleague to use your account or by using theirs. Separation of duties protects the record.
- A greyed or missing value is not a "No". Ask the record owner when in doubt.
- Do not retry a command many times when the result is uncertain. The page keeps the original request and tells you whether it was received.
