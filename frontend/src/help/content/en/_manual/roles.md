## Master (ship mode)
The Master handles the day's exceptions, departure readiness, daily records, crew and manning, technical readiness, safety and risk, and arrival and port activity (13.03 §2). The Master decides release, conditions or stop for departure; POSEIDON shows the reasons and the evidence but does not make that decision. Where crew data is critical and missing, the page never shows a false green.

## Tug Master (tug mode)
The Tug Master confirms the job context and the assigned tug; a shore assignment proposal never removes the onboard authority (13.03 §3). Crew readiness considers manning, credentials, competence, familiarization and work/rest together. Equipment readiness checks winch, towline, tow hook or chain stopper, emergency release, fire-fighting and critical defect evidence. The towage plan, risk and briefing are completed before any READY decision, and participants record their acknowledgement. Execution records start, suspend, resume, abort and complete with evidence.

## DPA / HSEQ
The DPA and HSEQ users watch critical events and overdue actions, govern risk templates and high-risk work, manage change, follow audit and inspection findings through action and verification, and judge effectiveness (13.03 §4). Verified does not mean effective. Confidential reports are restricted to people who need to know.

## Chief Engineer / Technical Superintendent
The Chief Engineer and Technical Superintendent keep the equipment hierarchy and critical equipment register current, run planned and unplanned work orders, running hours, measurements, postponements and verification, record safeguards and restrictions for critical defects, link service and engineer reports to work order and equipment history, watch critical spare thresholds and link the drydock scope to defects, work orders, certificates and surveys (13.03 §5).

## Crew / OOW / Engineer
Crew members carry out the tasks assigned to them from Today, attach evidence where needed and never change someone else's verified record (13.03 §6). Deck and engine daily logs are kept in real time and in order. Briefing acknowledgements, familiarization and training are completed in the right role context. While offline, check the sync state; when a conflict appears, do not assume your own record wins.

## Tug Operations Coordinator / Shore Operations
The coordinator structures each request with Job Intake (assisted vessel, job type, time window, required capability), watches conflicts and readiness on the Job Board and proposes tug assignments (13.03 §7). The coordinator never overrides the Tug Master's onboard safety decision. For finished jobs, evidence completeness, downtime, events and history are followed up.

## Regulatory / Assurance
The regulatory user registers official, versioned sources with their effective date and lifecycle, evaluates applicability with its context inputs and leaves the result as REVIEW_REQUIRED when critical data is unknown (13.03 §8). No orphan record is left in the requirement → control → evidence → verification chain. Class, flag, company and local rules are never assumed to replace each other.

## System / Tenant Admin
The tenant administrator assigns users, roles and company, vessel and record scopes with least privilege, applies sign-in and multi-factor policies through the central identity provider, audits the start, end and scope of any delegation, and publishes configuration in a controlled way instead of correcting production data directly (13.03 §9). Product owner and support / engineering pages (pilot gates, system health) are listed in this chapter too.
