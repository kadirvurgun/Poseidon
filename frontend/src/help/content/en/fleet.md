## What this page is for
"Fleet" is the **list of ships and tugs in your scope**. It answers: "Which vessels can I access, and where are a vessel's record, regulatory profile, compliance passport and attachments?" The page shows the vessel record and recorded information; operational readiness is evaluated separately.

## Who uses it
- **Master / Tug Master / Chief Engineer:** to select their own vessel and go to its workspaces (equipment and maintenance, crew roster) (13.03 §2, §3, §5).
- **Shore Operations:** to find vessels in the fleet and review their records.
- **Tenant Admin:** to create and edit vessel records (13.03 §9).

## Before you start
- In the top context bar, check that the **right company** is selected (13.03 §1). The list shows only vessels in your scope.
- You need read access to see the list. "New record" and "Edit" appear only for vessels you have write access to.

## Step by step
1. In the summary cards at the top, see the number of listed vessels, ships, tugs and editable records on this page. **Counts cover this page only**.
2. Type in **Search by code or name**, choose "Ship" or "Tug" in **Vessel type** if needed, and press **Search**.
3. Press a vessel's name in the table. **Details** shows its code, name and type; the selected vessel appears as "Current vessel".
4. Open the vessel's profile with **Regulatory profile**, or its onboarding inputs with **Pilot vessel onboarding**.
5. Use **Digital compliance passport** to review recorded requirements, sources, the applicability review and unresolved blockers. Use **Back to vessel** to return.
6. Go to the vessel's workspaces with **Controlled import**, **Equipment and maintenance** or **Crew roster**.
7. In the lower section, switch between **Vessel attachments** and **Document drafts**.
8. If you have access, add a vessel with **New record** or change it with **Edit** and save. Use **Switch vessel** for another vessel.

## What the states mean
- **Read-only / Editable records:** your current access on this page. "Edit" is not shown on a read-only record.
- **This page only. Operational readiness is evaluated separately.:** the vessel composition chart gives no readiness information.
- **Review required · unverified freshness:** passport information is read from recorded sources and is not verified. The passport is not a compliance approval.
- **No current requirement rows returned:** this does not establish an empty obligation set.
- **Unknown / Unavailable:** the value is missing in the source or could not be read. This does not mean "Compliant" or "Ready".

## What you cannot do / caution
- This page never says a vessel is ready for operation or departure. The departure decision belongs to the Master and the towage decision to the Tug Master.
- A generic administrator cannot replace qualified source admission or an authorised human decision.
- If you leave with unsaved changes you are asked to confirm; changes are kept until you save or explicitly leave.
- Data shown while offline is unverified; reconnect and retry.

## If something goes wrong
- **You do not have access to this workspace:** check your role and vessel context; contact your tenant administrator if needed (13.03 §10).
- **No records found:** change your search or the vessel type filter.
- **Sign in to continue:** your session has ended; use "Sign in" again.
- **The service is unavailable:** use "Retry"; if it persists, report it to support with a screenshot, the time and your steps.
